# Threat Model

The benchmark models **synthetic, in-memory integrity and availability effects only**.

Included abstractions: measurement bias, gradual drift, replay, metadata-rewritten replay, freeze, coordinated measurement manipulation, command scaling, packet loss and latency.

Out of scope: malware, credential theft, protocol exploitation, real device discovery, vendor-specific commands, persistence mechanisms, physical intrusion and operational targeting. The goal is defensive scientific evaluation of cyber-physical reasoning.
