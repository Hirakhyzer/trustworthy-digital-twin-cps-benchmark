# PhD Research Alignment

## Central thesis direction

A reusable, uncertainty-aware and trust-aware digital-twin architecture can support cyberattack detection, fault discrimination and resilient recovery across heterogeneous cyber-physical systems.

## Candidate research questions

- **RQ1:** How much detector performance transfers across heterogeneous CPS when the evidence representation is standardized?
- **RQ2:** Can physics, temporal and cross-sensor evidence improve attack-vs-fault discrimination over residual thresholds alone?
- **RQ3:** When should a monitor trust telemetry versus the digital twin?
- **RQ4:** Does explicit uncertainty reduce false positives under model mismatch and legitimate operating changes?
- **RQ5:** How well do learned/calibrated thresholds transfer to an unseen domain?
- **RQ6:** Can trust-weighted reconstruction preserve state-estimation quality during cyber events?
- **RQ7:** Which evidence channels contribute most to detection of unknown or stealthy attacks?

The repository is designed to produce publishable experimental tables rather than only application demos.
