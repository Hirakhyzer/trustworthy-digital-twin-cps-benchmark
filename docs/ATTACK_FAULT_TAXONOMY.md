# Attack and Fault Taxonomy

## Cyber
Bias, drift, freeze, replay, rewritten replay, coordinated manipulation and abstract command manipulation.

## Fault
Sensor offset, physical process degradation/fault and communication degradation.

## Non-malicious confounders
Legitimate operational change and digital-twin model mismatch.

## Composite
Attack plus physical fault, and attack plus model mismatch.

A central benchmark objective is to avoid collapsing all deviations into a single `ANOMALY` label.
